Tanpura & Taal

Start the tanpura, choose the scale of your bansuri, and add a taal to play along.

Tanpura

Scale (your Sa)

The Sa is usually engraved on the flute, such as E or C♯. Not sure? Leave it on D for now.

Check the sound: start the tanpura, then tap Hear Sa. The two should blend into one steady note. Fine tune is only needed to match another instrument.

Taal

A synthesised tabla theka, tuned to your Sa. ✕ is sam (the first beat), a number is a tali (clap), ○ is khali (wave).
