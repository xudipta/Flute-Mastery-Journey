    Tabla taal player

    Practise with a synthesised tabla theka. The dayan is tuned to the Sa chosen in the tools bar. Sam is the first beat (✕), tali is a clap (number), khali is a wave (○).

    Finger-hole guide

    The bansuri has six finger holes — three for the left hand, three for the right. Tap a swar to see which holes are covered and hear it at your Sa.
